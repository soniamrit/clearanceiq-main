Important: Data Input Restrictions

Please read before starting your CMMC assessment

⛔ Do not enter Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI)

ClearanceIQ is a documentation assistance tool. It is not authorized to store, process, or transmit CUI. Your responses and generated documents pass through cloud infrastructure that does not meet the requirements for handling CUI.

What this means in practice
✕
Do not paste classified documents, CUI-marked files, or content extracted from ITAR/EAR-controlled systems.
✕
Do not enter real IP addresses, system credentials, network diagrams with live configurations, or personnel records.
✕
Do not describe the specific contents of CUI you handle — describe your handling processes and controls, not the information itself.
✓
Do enter descriptions of how you protect information, what security controls you have, and where your systems and processes have gaps.
✓
Do use general system descriptions ("we process CUI on Windows workstations in a dedicated VLAN") without embedding actual data or credentials.
