2. Description of Services

ClearanceIQ provides three tiers of cybersecurity compliance support services for US Department of Defense contractors subject to DFARS 252.204-7012 and CMMC 2.0 requirements:

  • ASSESS (Free): An anonymous, browser-based CMMC Phase 1 readiness assessment that provides an estimated SPRS score and gap summary. No account required.
  • CERTIFY ($999–$8,000): A guided 110-control documentation workflow that produces a System Security Plan (SSP), Plan of Action and Milestones (POA&M), and an Affirming Official attestation letter for SPRS submission, with practitioner review.
  • DEFEND ($8,000–$20,000/year): An AI-powered adversarial review service that challenges certification claims using a simulated DOJ Civil Cyber-Fraud Initiative investigator persona and produces a False Claims Risk Report prior to SPRS submission. Annual recertification included.
